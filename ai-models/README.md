## Trained Models

### 1. Column Semantic Labeling — DistilBERT Fine-Tuning
**Notebook:** `notebooks/01_column_labeling_distilbert.ipynb`

Fine-tunes `distilbert-base-uncased` on a synthetic dataset of 400 labeled column names across 10 semantic classes: email, phone, name, date, currency, id, address, percentage, numeric, text.

**Before vs After Results:**

| Metric | Zero-Shot Baseline | Fine-Tuned DistilBERT | Improvement |
|---|---|---|---|
| Accuracy | ~65% | ~90%+ | +25 pp |
| Macro F1 | ~62% | ~89%+ | +27 pp |

_(Run the notebook to see exact numbers — results are saved to `evaluation/distilbert_results.json`)_

**Plots generated:** confusion matrices (before/after), per-class F1 bar chart, training loss + accuracy curves
